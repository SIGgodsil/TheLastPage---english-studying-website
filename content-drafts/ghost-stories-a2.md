# The Last Page — 10 Ghost Stories (A2 drafts)

Nov 25, 2026 · @Benson

## Cách đọc bản nháp

Đây là bản nháp văn xuôi tiếng Anh trình độ A2 của 10 truyện ma, mỗi truyện khoảng 450–550 từ. Mục đích của bản này là để duyệt cốt truyện trước, sau đó mới chuyển sang kịch bản chat.

Mỗi truyện chia 4 trang, mỗi trang tương đương 12–15 tin nhắn khi chuyển sang chat. Mỗi trang kết thúc bằng một cliffhanger. Trang 4 dừng ngay trước cái kết, để người học tự viết ở The Last Page.

Mỗi truyện gồm 6 phần:

- **Thông tin:** cặp, chủ đề, nguồn gốc, mức độ sợ (1–3)
- **Tóm tắt** bằng tiếng Việt
- **Page 1–4:** truyện tiếng Anh A2
- **The Last Page:** câu hỏi để người học viết đoạn kết
- **Kết gốc:** chỉ để người viết tham khảo, không hiện cho người học
- **Từ vựng chính:** 10 từ, kèm nghĩa tiếng Việt

**Quy tắc ngôn ngữ đã áp dụng:**

- Câu 6–10 từ, chủ yếu dùng thì quá khứ đơn và hiện tại đơn
- Mẫu câu If I had…, I would… chỉ xuất hiện như câu cố định để người học dùng lại
- Gợi sự sợ hãi, không tả máu me
- Toàn bộ là lời văn mới, không chép nguyên tác hay các bản dịch hiện đại

## 1. The Monkey's Paw

**Cặp 1 – Người chết trở về** · W. W. Jacobs, 1902 · Mức sợ: 3/3

Một gia đình có được bàn tay khỉ ban ba điều ước. Điều ước nào cũng thành sự thật, theo cách khủng khiếp nhất.

### Page 1 — A Visitor in the Rain

It was a cold, rainy night. Mr. White, Mrs. White and their son Herbert sat by the fire. Someone knocked at the door. It was Sergeant-Major Morris, an old friend. He was back from India.

Morris told them strange stories. Then he took something small out of his pocket. It was a dry, brown paw.

"This is a monkey's paw," he said. "An old man put a spell on it. Three people can have three wishes."

"Did you make your wishes?" Herbert asked.

"Yes," Morris said quietly. His face was white.

Suddenly, he threw the paw into the fire. Mr. White quickly pulled it out.

"Throw it away," Morris said. "If you wish, be careful what you wish for."

When Morris left, Mr. White held the paw in his hand. And he smiled.

### Page 2 — The First Wish

Herbert laughed. "Wish for money, Dad! Two hundred pounds!"

Mr. White held up the paw. "I wish for two hundred pounds," he said.

Then he cried out and dropped it. "It moved! It moved in my hand like a snake!"

They looked at the floor. The paw did not move. And there was no money.

"See? It's just a story," Herbert said with a smile.

That night, Herbert sat alone by the fire. In the flames, he saw a face. It was a horrible monkey face, and it looked at him. Herbert went to bed fast.

In the morning, the sun was bright. Everything felt normal. Herbert went to work at the factory.

In the afternoon, a man in a dark suit stopped at their gate.

### Page 3 — Two Hundred Pounds

The man was from Herbert's factory. He looked at the floor.

"I am very sorry," he said. "There was an accident. Herbert was caught in the machine. He is dead."

Mrs. White screamed. Mr. White could not speak.

"The company is very sorry," the man said. "They want to give you some money. Two hundred pounds."

They buried Herbert two miles from the house. Now the house was quiet and empty.

Ten days later, in the middle of the night, Mrs. White sat up in bed.

"The paw!" she cried. "We have two more wishes! Wish our boy back!"

"No," Mr. White said. "He was in the machine. You don't want to see him."

"Wish him back!" she shouted.

With shaking hands, Mr. White held up the paw.

### Page 4 — Knock, Knock

"I wish my son was alive again," he said.

They waited. Nothing happened. The candle went out. The house was dark and very cold.

Then they heard it. Knock. A soft knock at the front door.

Knock. Knock.

"It's Herbert!" Mrs. White cried. "It's my boy!" She ran down the stairs.

"Wait!" Mr. White shouted. "Don't open the door!"

The knocking got louder and louder. Mrs. White pulled a chair to the door. She tried to reach the top lock.

Mr. White got down on the floor. He looked for the paw in the dark.

Knock! Knock! KNOCK!

His fingers touched something small and dry. The paw. Then he heard the top lock open…

### The Last Page

Điều ước thứ ba của Mr. White là gì? Khi cánh cửa mở ra, chuyện gì xảy ra? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I had the last wish, I would wish…

**Kết gốc:** Ông White ước điều thứ ba. Tiếng gõ cửa dừng lại. Bà White mở cửa, nhưng con đường vắng tanh.

**Từ vựng chính:** knock (gõ cửa) · paw (bàn chân thú) · wish (điều ước) · spell (bùa phép) · careful (cẩn thận) · accident (tai nạn) · machine (máy móc) · alive (còn sống) · candle (nến) · lock (ổ khóa)

## 2. The Girl from Nam Xuong

**Cặp 1 – Người chết trở về** · Chuyện người con gái Nam Xương, Nguyễn Dữ, thế kỷ 16 · Mức sợ: 2/3

Người chồng hay ghen tin lời đứa con nói về "người cha đêm nào cũng đến", rồi đổ oan cho vợ. Khi biết sự thật thì đã quá muộn, và người vợ chỉ còn hiện về trên dòng sông.

### Page 1 — The Shadow on the Wall

Long ago, in Nam Xuong village, there was a kind young woman. Her name was Vu Nuong. She was beautiful and good to everyone.

She married a man called Truong Sinh. He was rich, but he was jealous. He did not trust people easily.

One day, a war started. Truong Sinh had to go and fight. Vu Nuong cried at the gate. "Please come home safe," she said.

A few months later, she had a baby boy, Dan. She took care of the baby. She also took care of her husband's old mother. When the old woman died, Vu Nuong buried her with love.

Every night, little Dan asked, "Where is my father?"

One night, Vu Nuong smiled. She pointed at her shadow on the wall. "Look," she said. "That is your father."

### Page 2 — "You Are Not My Father"

Three years later, the war ended. Truong Sinh came home.

His mother was dead, and he was very sad. He took little Dan to visit her grave.

"Come here, son," he said. "I am your father."

But Dan cried and pushed him away.

"You are not my father! My father comes every night. When Mother walks, he walks. When Mother sits, he sits. But he never talks to me."

Truong Sinh stopped. His heart went cold.

"Another man comes to my house every night?" he thought.

He did not ask any questions. He walked home fast. His face was dark and angry.

### Page 3 — The River

At home, Truong Sinh shouted at Vu Nuong. "Who is the man that comes every night?"

"There is no man," she cried. "Please, listen to me!"

But he did not listen. He told her to leave. The neighbors tried to help her. He did not listen to them either.

Vu Nuong walked to the Hoang Giang river. She looked up at the sky. "I did nothing wrong," she said softly. Then she walked into the river. The water closed over her, and she was gone.

That night, Truong Sinh sat alone with Dan by a small lamp.

Suddenly, Dan pointed at the wall. "Look! Father is here!"

Truong Sinh looked. It was his own shadow on the wall.

Now he understood. But it was too late.

### Page 4 — The Lady on the River

Months later, a man from the village, Phan Lang, had an accident at sea. When he woke up, he was in a palace under the water. The Water Queen saved him.

In the palace, he saw a lady. It was Vu Nuong! She lived with the river spirits now.

"Please come home," Phan Lang said. "Your husband knows the truth now."

Vu Nuong gave him a golden hairpin. "Give this to my husband. Tell him to make an altar by the river. Then I will come."

Truong Sinh made an altar by the river. He waited there with Dan.

Suddenly, a beautiful boat appeared on the water. Its flags were bright in the wind. A lady stood on the boat. It was Vu Nuong.

She looked at her husband and her son. Then she opened her mouth to speak…

### The Last Page

Vũ Nương nói gì với chồng và con? Nàng có trở về không? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were Vu Nuong, I would say…

**Kết gốc:** Nàng cảm ơn chồng, nhưng nói rằng mình không thể trở về nhân gian nữa. Rồi bóng nàng mờ dần và biến mất trên mặt sông.

**Từ vựng chính:** shadow (cái bóng) · jealous (ghen tuông) · trust (tin tưởng) · war (chiến tranh) · grave (mộ) · neighbor (hàng xóm) · river (sông) · truth (sự thật) · palace (cung điện) · altar (bàn thờ)

## 3. The Upper Berth

**Cặp 2 – Ma nước** · F. Marion Crawford, 1886 · Mức sợ: 3/3

Trên một con tàu biển, ai ngủ ở buồng 105 cũng biến mất. Brisbane không tin, cho đến khi ông chạm vào thứ đang nằm trên giường tầng trên.

### Page 1 — Cabin 105

Brisbane was a rich young man. He traveled across the ocean on a big ship called the Kamtschatka.

"My cabin is 105," he told the steward.

The steward's face changed. "Oh… 105," he said quietly. He said nothing more.

In cabin 105, there were two beds, one above the other. A man already slept in the upper bed. He was quiet and looked very tired.

In the middle of the night, Brisbane woke up. The man jumped down from the upper bed and ran out. The door banged.

The cabin was cold. It smelled of sea water.

In the morning, Brisbane looked for the man. He was not on the ship. And the small round window was wide open.

### Page 2 — The Window

Brisbane went to the captain.

The captain looked serious. "Cabin 105 again," he said. "On my last three trips, someone from that cabin disappeared at sea."

Brisbane laughed. "I'm not afraid. I will stay there."

The steward closed the round window. He screwed it shut, very tight. "Now no one can open it," he said.

That night, Brisbane lay in his bed. The air was cold and wet. He smelled the sea again.

He looked at the window. It was open.

Then he heard something above him. Something moved in the upper bed.

### Page 3 — The Cold Hand

Brisbane stood up. He put his hand into the upper bed.

He touched something. It was an arm. It was wet, cold and soft, like a dead man's arm.

The thing pushed him hard. Brisbane fell to the floor. A dark shape ran out of the cabin.

He ran after it, but it was gone.

He went back and touched the upper bed. It was wet with sea water.

In the morning, he told the captain everything. This time, the captain did not laugh.

"I want to see it," the captain said. "Tonight, we will wait in 105 together."

### Page 4 — The Thing in the Upper Bed

At night, they waited in the dark cabin. The captain held a lamp.

At two o'clock, the room got very cold. They smelled the sea.

Slowly, the round window opened by itself. Something moved in the upper bed.

The captain held up the lamp. In the upper bed, there was a face. It was white and wet. Its eyes were open, but they did not move.

Brisbane jumped up and grabbed it. It was very strong and very cold. It pulled him toward the open window.

"Help me!" he shouted.

The captain ran toward them. Then the lamp fell to the floor, and everything went black…

### The Last Page

Thứ nằm trên giường tầng trên là gì? Brisbane và thuyền trưởng thoát ra bằng cách nào? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were Brisbane, I would…

**Kết gốc (đại ý):** Brisbane vật lộn với thứ đó và bị thương, còn nó biến mất. Sau chuyến đi, Brisbane không bao giờ đi con tàu đó nữa.

**Từ vựng chính:** ship (con tàu) · cabin (buồng tàu) · steward (tiếp viên tàu) · upper (ở trên) · captain (thuyền trưởng) · disappear (biến mất) · smell (ngửi thấy, có mùi) · wet (ướt) · shape (hình dáng, bóng) · grab (chộp lấy)

## 4. The Water Ghost (Ma da)

**Cặp 2 – Ma nước** · Truyền thuyết dân gian Việt Nam, nhân vật mới sáng tác · Mức sợ: 3/3

Một năm sau khi cậu bạn thân Hùng chết đuối, Nam bắt đầu nghe thấy tiếng Hùng gọi mình xuống sông bơi. Rồi đến lượt em gái Lan mất tích.

### Page 1 — The Red Shirt

Nam lived in a small village by a big river. In summer, the children swam there every day.

But Grandma always said, "Don't swim at noon. And never swim alone."

"Why?" Nam asked.

"Because of the ma da," Grandma said. "When a person drowns, he becomes a water ghost. He is cold and lonely. He waits under the water for someone to take his place."

Nam laughed. "That's just an old story, Grandma."

Last summer, Nam's best friend Hung drowned in that river. Nobody ever found him.

One hot afternoon, Nam went to the river alone. On a big rock by the water, he saw something red.

It was a shirt. Hung's red shirt. And it was wet.

### Page 2 — The Hand Under the Water

Nam's heart beat fast. "Someone is playing a joke," he thought.

He jumped into the water. The river was warm. But in the middle, the water was suddenly ice cold.

A hand held his ankle. It pulled him down.

Nam kicked hard. He swam to the bank and climbed out. On his ankle, there were five dark marks, like fingers.

That night, Nam could not sleep. Outside, he heard wet footsteps. Drip. Drip. Drip.

Then a voice whispered at the window. "Nam… come and swim with me. I'm so cold."

It was Hung's voice.

Grandma held Nam's hand. "He is looking for someone to take his place," she said.

### Page 3 — Lan

The next evening, Nam's little sister Lan was missing. Everyone looked for her. Nam ran to the river with a lamp.

On the bank, he found Lan's small pink slippers.

"Lan!" he shouted.

Then he saw her. She was standing in the river. The water was up to her waist. She was smiling, but her eyes were closed.

She walked slowly into deeper water. Next to her, there was a dark shadow. It was a boy in a red shirt.

"Hung, stop!" Nam shouted.

The shadow turned and looked at him. Then it pulled Lan under the water.

Nam dropped the lamp and jumped into the dark river.

### Page 4 — "One of You Must Stay"

Under the water, it was black and very cold. Nam found Lan's hand. He pulled hard.

But another hand held her. It was Hung's hand.

Nam heard a voice in his head. "I'm cold, Nam. I'm alone. Nobody remembers me. One of you must stay with me."

Nam's chest hurt. He needed air. Lan was not moving.

He remembered Grandma's words: "He is cold and lonely."

Nam looked into Hung's empty eyes. He was not angry anymore. He was sad for his friend.

Then Nam made a choice. He opened his mouth and spoke to his friend…

### The Last Page

Nam nói gì với Hùng? Chuyện gì xảy ra với Nam, Lan và Hùng? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were Nam, I would say…

**Kết gốc:** Truyền thuyết dân gian không có cái kết cố định. Theo tín ngưỡng, ma da chỉ được siêu thoát khi có người chết thay, hoặc khi người sống cúng giỗ, giải oan. Truyện đã cài sẵn hướng thứ hai: tình bạn và sự thương nhớ giải thoát cho Hùng.

**Từ vựng chính:** river (sông) · drown (chết đuối) · ghost (ma) · lonely (cô đơn) · ankle (mắt cá chân) · bank (bờ sông) · footstep (tiếng bước chân) · whisper (thì thầm) · missing (mất tích) · slipper (dép)

## 5. The Legend of Sleepy Hollow

**Cặp 3 – Gặp ma giữa đêm** · Washington Irving, 1820 · Mức sợ: 2/3

Anh thầy giáo nhút gan Ichabod cưỡi ngựa về nhà lúc nửa đêm, và bị Kỵ sĩ không đầu đuổi theo trên con đường vắng.

### Page 1 — The Headless Horseman

Sleepy Hollow was a quiet village in a deep valley. People there loved ghost stories.

The most famous story was about the Headless Horseman. He was a soldier who lost his head in a war. Now he rode his black horse through the valley every night. He was looking for his head.

The village teacher was Ichabod Crane. He was tall and very thin, with big ears and long arms. He loved ghost stories, but he was afraid of the dark.

Ichabod loved a girl named Katrina. She was beautiful, and her father was rich.

But a big, strong man also loved Katrina. His name was Brom Bones, and he always laughed at Ichabod.

One day, a letter came. Katrina's family invited Ichabod to a big party.

### Page 2 — The Party

The party was wonderful. There was music, dancing and lots of food.

Late at night, people told ghost stories by the fire.

"I saw the Headless Horseman," Brom said. "I raced him to the old bridge by the church. Ghosts can't cross that bridge. So he disappeared in a ball of fire!"

Everyone laughed, but Ichabod felt cold.

After the party, Ichabod asked Katrina to marry him. She said no.

At midnight, he rode home alone on his old horse, Gunpowder. The road was dark and empty. The trees made strange shapes in the night.

Then he heard a sound behind him. It was another horse.

### Page 3 — The Rider With No Head

Ichabod looked back. A huge rider on a black horse was behind him. He was big and dark, and he did not speak.

Ichabod rode faster. The rider rode faster too.

Ichabod rode slower. The rider rode slower too.

Then the moon came out, and Ichabod saw the rider clearly. There was no head on his shoulders. His head was on the saddle in front of him!

Ichabod screamed. "Go, Gunpowder, go!"

He remembered Brom's story. "The bridge! Ghosts can't cross the bridge!"

He saw the old bridge by the church. He rode over it as fast as he could.

Then he turned around to look.

### Page 4 — The Bridge

Ichabod stopped on the other side of the bridge. He breathed hard. "I'm safe," he said.

But the Headless Horseman did not disappear. He stopped at the end of the bridge. His black horse kicked the ground. Fire came out of its nose.

Slowly, the rider stood up in the saddle. He held his head high in the air.

"No… please…" Ichabod whispered.

The rider threw his head, hard, straight at Ichabod.

Ichabod saw something big and round flying toward him in the dark. He could not move…

### The Last Page

Chuyện gì xảy ra với Ichabod? Kỵ sĩ không đầu thật ra là ai? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were Ichabod, I would…

**Kết gốc:** Sáng hôm sau, ngựa Gunpowder về một mình. Người ta tìm thấy mũ của Ichabod bên đường, cạnh một quả bí ngô vỡ. Ichabod biến mất mãi mãi. Brom cưới Katrina, và hễ ai nhắc đến quả bí ngô là anh ta cười rất to. Kết gốc vốn bỏ ngỏ nên rất hợp để viết lại.

**Từ vựng chính:** valley (thung lũng) · headless (không đầu) · rider (người cưỡi ngựa) · soldier (người lính) · afraid (sợ hãi) · invite (mời) · bridge (cây cầu) · cross (băng qua) · saddle (yên ngựa) · throw (ném)

## 6. The Kapok Tree

**Cặp 3 – Gặp ma giữa đêm** · Chuyện cây gạo, Nguyễn Dữ, thế kỷ 16 · Mức sợ: 2/3

Chàng thương nhân Trình Trung Ngộ đêm nào cũng gặp cô gái xinh đẹp Nhị Khanh. Một đêm, chàng lần theo nàng về nhà, và "nhà" nàng là một cỗ quan tài dưới gốc cây gạo.

### Page 1 — The Girl at Night

Long ago, a young merchant named Trinh Trung Ngo lived near the capital. His house was by the river.

One evening, he saw a beautiful girl on the road. She wore a long white dress. A young servant walked behind her.

"My name is Nhi Khanh," she said with a soft smile. "I live near here, alone."

After that, Nhi Khanh came to his house every night. They talked, laughed and drank tea together.

But every night, she left before the sun came up. She never said where her house was.

Ngo's friends looked worried. "Your face is so pale," they said. "Are you sick?"

One night, when Nhi Khanh left, Ngo quietly followed her.

### Page 2 — The House Under the Kapok Tree

Nhi Khanh walked fast in the dark. Ngo followed her far from the village.

She stopped at an old, empty place. There was a huge kapok tree there. Its branches looked like long black arms.

Under the tree, there was a small, broken house. Nhi Khanh went inside. Then the light went out.

Ngo waited until morning. Then he went into the house.

There was no bed and no table. There was only a coffin.

On the coffin, he read two words: "Nhi Khanh." She was dead. She died many years ago.

Suddenly, the lid of the coffin moved. A pale hand came out and held his sleeve.

### Page 3 — The Call in the Night

Ngo screamed and pulled his sleeve away. He ran all the way home.

After that day, he was very sick. He did not eat, and he did not sleep.

Every night, he heard a soft voice outside his door. "Ngo… why did you leave me? Come to me…"

His friends locked the door. They stayed with him day and night.

But one night, his friends fell asleep. Ngo stood up. His eyes were empty, like a sleepwalker's. He opened the door and walked into the dark.

The next morning, his friends ran to the kapok tree. They found Ngo in the broken house. He was holding the coffin, and he was dead.

### Page 4 — Two Ghosts

After that, strange things happened near the kapok tree.

On dark, rainy nights, people saw two ghosts there: a young man and a girl in white. They held hands and laughed. Sometimes they sang sad songs.

Anyone who saw them became very sick. Nobody walked near the tree at night.

The villagers asked an old priest for help. He had magic papers and a long sword.

On the night of the full moon, the old priest walked to the kapok tree. He burned his magic papers. The wind blew hard.

The two ghosts appeared and screamed at him.

The old priest raised his sword. The kapok tree began to shake…

### The Last Page

Ông đạo sĩ làm gì? Hai hồn ma có được siêu thoát không? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were the priest, I would…

**Kết gốc (đại ý):** Đạo sĩ dùng bùa gọi thiên binh đến bắt hai hồn ma. Chiếc quan tài bị đập vỡ và ném xuống sông. Từ đó, cây gạo không còn ma quỷ nữa.

**Từ vựng chính:** merchant (thương nhân) · servant (người hầu) · pale (nhợt nhạt) · follow (đi theo) · kapok tree (cây gạo) · coffin (quan tài) · lid (nắp) · sleeve (tay áo) · priest (đạo sĩ, thầy pháp) · sword (thanh gươm)

## 7. Bluebeard

**Cặp 4 – Vết máu** · Charles Perrault, 1697 · Mức sợ: 2/3

Người vợ trẻ của Râu Xanh được mở mọi căn phòng, trừ một căn. Cô mở nó, và vết máu trên chiếc chìa khóa không bao giờ rửa sạch được.

### Page 1 — The Little Key

Long ago, there was a very rich man. He had big houses, gold and beautiful horses. But he had a blue beard, and people were afraid of him.

He married many times before. Nobody knew where his wives were now.

One day, Bluebeard asked a young woman, Anna, to marry him. Her family was poor. At first she was afraid, but he was kind to her. So she said yes.

A month later, Bluebeard said, "I must go on a long trip." He gave Anna a big bunch of keys.

"You can open every room," he said. "But never open the small room at the end of the long hall. This little key is for that room. If you open it, you will be very sorry."

That night, Anna stood in front of the small door. The little key was in her hand.

### Page 2 — The Secret Room

"Just one look," Anna thought. Her hands were shaking. She opened the door.

The room was dark, and the windows were closed. At first, she saw nothing.

Then she looked at the floor. It was dark and wet.

Along the wall, there were women in long dresses. They did not move. They did not breathe. They were Bluebeard's wives.

Anna screamed. The little key fell onto the wet floor.

She picked it up, closed the door and ran to her room.

She washed the key. There was a red spot on it. She washed it again and again, but the red spot did not go away. It was a magic key.

Then she heard horses outside. Bluebeard was home early.

### Page 3 — Sister Anne

"Give me my keys," Bluebeard said.

Anna gave him the keys. Her hands were cold.

He looked at the little key. He saw the red spot.

"You opened the door," he said quietly. "Now you will join them."

"Please," Anna cried. "Give me fifteen minutes to pray."

"Fifteen minutes," he said. "Not one more."

Anna ran up to the high tower. Her sister Anne was there.

"Our brothers are coming to visit today!" Anna said. "Sister Anne, do you see anyone on the road?"

Anne looked out the window. "I see only the sun and the dust."

Downstairs, Bluebeard shouted, "Come down now!" He started to climb the stairs with a long sword.

### Page 4 — Dust on the Road

"Sister Anne, do you see anyone coming?" Anna cried.

"I see a cloud of dust!" Anne said. "But it's only a group of sheep."

Bluebeard's footsteps came closer and closer. The door opened.

He pulled Anna down the stairs and raised his long sword.

"Sister Anne!" Anna shouted. "Please, look again!"

"Two riders!" Anne cried. "Two riders on the road! They're coming fast!"

At that moment, someone banged hard on the castle gate.

Bluebeard stopped. He looked at the gate. Then he looked at Anna, and he lifted his sword higher…

### The Last Page

Ai đang đến? Chuyện gì xảy ra với Anna và Râu Xanh? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were Anna, I would…

**Kết gốc:** Hai người anh trai xông vào kịp lúc và đánh bại Râu Xanh. Anna thừa kế toàn bộ tài sản, giúp đỡ gia đình, và sau đó cưới một người tốt.

**Từ vựng chính:** beard (râu) · rich (giàu) · wife, wives (vợ) · trip (chuyến đi) · key (chìa khóa) · hall (hành lang) · secret (bí mật) · spot (vết) · tower (tòa tháp) · dust (bụi)

## 8. The Legend of the Mosquito

**Cặp 4 – Vết máu** · Sự tích con muỗi, truyện cổ tích Việt Nam · Mức sợ: 2/3

Người chồng nhỏ ba giọt máu để cứu sống vợ. Nhưng người vợ bỏ theo một lái buôn giàu có, và anh đòi lại ba giọt máu đó.

### Page 1 — The Coffin on the Boat

Long ago, there was a poor farmer named Ngoc Tam. He worked hard in the fields every day.

His wife, Nhan Diep, was very beautiful. But she did not like to work. She only wanted nice clothes and good food.

One day, Nhan Diep suddenly got sick and died.

Ngoc Tam cried for days. He did not want to bury her. He sold his house and his fields. He bought a small boat and put her coffin on it.

"I will find a way to bring you back," he said.

He sailed for many weeks. One evening, he arrived at a green mountain by the sea.

An old man with a long white beard was waiting for him. "I can bring your wife back," the old man said. "But you may be sorry."

### Page 2 — Three Drops of Blood

"Please," Ngoc Tam said. "I will do anything."

"Cut your finger," the old man said. "Let three drops of blood fall on her."

Ngoc Tam cut his finger. One drop. Two drops. Three drops.

Slowly, Nhan Diep opened her eyes. Her cheeks turned pink. She sat up and smiled.

"Be careful," the old man said. "She does not love you like you love her."

But Ngoc Tam did not listen. He took his wife home on the boat.

On the way, they stopped at a busy market. Next to their boat, there was a big boat. It belonged to a rich merchant. The merchant smiled at Nhan Diep.

Ngoc Tam went to buy food. When he came back, his wife and the big boat were gone.

### Page 3 — The Rich Man's House

Ngoc Tam looked for his wife everywhere. He walked from town to town for months.

At last, he found her in the rich merchant's big house. She wore silk clothes and gold rings.

"Nhan Diep," he said. "Please come home with me."

She laughed. "Home? To your poor little house? No. I like this life."

"I gave you my blood," he said. "I gave you everything."

"I didn't ask for it," she said coldly.

Ngoc Tam looked at her for a long time. His heart was cold now.

"Then give me back my three drops of blood," he said.

### Page 4 — The Last Drop

Nhan Diep laughed again. "Is that all? Fine."

She took a small knife from the table and cut her finger.

One drop of blood fell to the floor. Her face turned white.

Two drops. Her hands began to shake. "What… what is happening?" she whispered.

Ngoc Tam did not answer. He only watched.

Nhan Diep held her finger tightly, but the blood did not stop.

The third drop slowly grew at the end of her finger…

### The Last Page

Khi giọt máu thứ ba rơi xuống, chuyện gì xảy ra? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were Ngoc Tam, I would…

**Kết gốc:** Giọt thứ ba rơi xuống, Nhan Diệp ngã xuống và chết. Hồn nàng không cam lòng, hóa thành con muỗi, ngày đêm đi hút máu người để đòi lại ba giọt máu đã mất.

**Từ vựng chính:** farmer (nông dân) · field (cánh đồng) · bury (chôn cất) · sail (đi thuyền) · drop (giọt) · blood (máu) · finger (ngón tay) · market (chợ) · silk (lụa) · sorry (hối hận)

## 9. The Toll-House

**Cặp 5 – Nơi bị ma ám** · W. W. Jacobs, 1907 · Mức sợ: 3/3

Bốn người bạn cá cược ngủ một đêm trong ngôi nhà không ai ở được đến sáng. Mỗi trang là một mốc giờ: 10 giờ tối, nửa đêm, 2 giờ sáng, và trước lúc bình minh.

### Page 1 — The Bet (10 p.m.)

"Nobody can stay one night in the old toll-house," said Jack Barnes. "Everyone runs away before morning."

His three friends, Lester, Meagar and White, laughed. "Ghosts aren't real," said White.

"Then let's stay there tonight," Jack said. "All four of us."

The toll-house was an old, empty house by a lonely road. The walls were gray, and the windows were dirty.

At ten o'clock, they opened the heavy door. Inside, it was cold and dark. It smelled of dust.

They sat in the kitchen with candles, cards and hot coffee.

"See?" said Lester. "Nothing here."

Then, upstairs, a door closed. Bang.

### Page 2 — Footsteps (Midnight)

They looked at each other.

"It's just the wind," White said. But his voice was quiet.

At midnight, they took the candles and went upstairs. They checked every room. All the rooms were empty. All the windows were closed.

They came back down. Behind them, on the stairs, they heard footsteps. Slow, heavy footsteps.

They turned around. Nobody was there. The footsteps stopped.

Then Lester felt a cold hand on his shoulder.

He turned around quickly. Nobody was behind him.

### Page 3 — The Open Door (2 a.m.)

"Let's sit together," Jack said. "Let's hold hands. Nobody goes anywhere alone."

They sat in a circle in the kitchen. Nobody talked.

At two o'clock, the kitchen door opened slowly by itself.

Suddenly, Jack stood up. His eyes looked strange and empty.

"Jack! Sit down!" Meagar said.

But Jack did not hear him. He walked toward the open door, like a sleepwalker.

His friends grabbed his arms. Jack was very strong. He pulled and pulled.

Then a cold wind blew, and all the candles went out.

In the dark, Jack's hand slipped away.

### Page 4 — Before the Sun (Dawn)

"Jack! Jack, where are you?" White shouted.

Lester found a match and lit one candle. Jack was gone.

They heard a laugh upstairs. It was Jack's voice. But it was not Jack's laugh.

The three friends ran up the stairs with the small candle.

The hall was long and dark. At the end of the hall, a man stood with his back to them.

"Jack?" Lester said softly.

Outside, the sky was getting light. Dawn was only one minute away.

The man began to turn around, very slowly. And they saw his face…

### The Last Page

Chuyện gì đã xảy ra với Jack? Ba người bạn có cứu được Jack và thoát ra trước bình minh không? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were Jack's friend, I would…

**Kết gốc (đại ý):** Barnes bị ngôi nhà "gọi" đi và được tìm thấy đã chết ở cầu thang ngay trước bình minh. Ngôi nhà vẫn bị ma ám. Lưu ý: bản gốc kể rằng những người từng ở lại trong nhà đều đã tự tử. Bản chuyển thể thay chi tiết này bằng "không ai ở được đến sáng" để tránh chủ đề nhạy cảm.

**Từ vựng chính:** bet (cá cược) · empty (trống rỗng) · heavy (nặng) · upstairs (tầng trên) · footstep (tiếng bước chân) · shoulder (vai) · circle (vòng tròn) · sleepwalker (người mộng du) · match (que diêm) · dawn (bình minh)

## 10. The Temple Judge

**Cặp 5 – Nơi bị ma ám** · Chuyện chức phán sự đền Tản Viên, Nguyễn Dữ, thế kỷ 16 · Mức sợ: 2/3

Ngô Tử Văn đốt ngôi đền của một hồn ma tướng giặc độc ác, rồi bị kéo xuống âm phủ để đối chất trước Diêm Vương.

### Page 1 — The Burning Temple

Long ago, there was a young man named Ngo Tu Van. He was honest and brave. He was not afraid of anything.

Near his village, there was an old temple. An evil ghost lived there. He was the ghost of an enemy general who died in a war long ago.

The ghost took the temple from the good Earth God. Now he made people sick and stole their animals. Everyone was afraid of him.

"This is wrong," Van said.

One day, he washed, prayed to the sky and set fire to the temple. Everyone watched it burn.

That night, Van felt sick. When he opened his eyes, a tall man stood by his bed. He wore a helmet and soldier's clothes.

"You burned my house," the man said angrily. "Build it again, or you will die!"

### Page 2 — The Old Man

"No," Van said. "You are an evil ghost. I will not help you."

The tall man laughed. "Then I will take you to the King of the Underworld." And he disappeared.

The next day, an old man came to see Van. His clothes were old and simple.

"I am the Earth God," the old man said. "That ghost took my temple. Thank you for helping me."

"Will the ghost hurt me?" Van asked.

"He will take you to the court of the dead," the Earth God said. "He will tell lies. But you must tell the truth. If the king doesn't believe you, ask him to check with the Tan Vien temple."

That night, two ghost soldiers came. They grabbed Van's arms and pulled him into the darkness.

### Page 3 — The Court of the Dead

Van woke up in a big, dark hall. There were ghosts and soldiers everywhere. On a high chair sat the King of the Underworld.

The ghost general stood in front of the king. "This man burned my temple!" he cried. "He is a bad man. Punish him!"

The king looked at Van. "Is this true?"

"Yes, I burned the temple," Van said. "But this ghost is evil. He stole the temple from the Earth God, and he hurt many people."

"Liar!" the ghost shouted.

The king was angry. "Who is telling the truth?"

Van stood tall. "Please, ask the Tan Vien temple. They know everything."

The king sent a messenger to Tan Vien. The ghost general's face turned white.

### Page 4 — The King's Decision

Everyone waited in the dark hall. Nobody spoke.

The ghost general looked at the door again and again. His hands were shaking.

After a long time, the big doors opened. The messenger came in with a letter from Tan Vien.

The king opened the letter and read it slowly. His face did not change.

He looked at the ghost general. Then he looked at Van.

The hall was silent.

The king stood up and raised his hand…

### The Last Page

Diêm Vương quyết định thế nào? Chuyện gì xảy ra với Văn và hồn ma tướng giặc? Mẫu câu gợi ý: If I were the king, I would…

**Kết gốc:** Thư từ đền Tản Viên chứng minh Văn nói thật. Hồn ma tướng giặc bị trị tội và đày xuống ngục sâu. Văn được trả về dương gian và sống lại. Một tháng sau, nhờ Thổ thần tiến cử, Văn nhận chức phán sự (quan xử án) ở đền Tản Viên.

**Từ vựng chính:** honest (thật thà) · brave (dũng cảm) · temple (ngôi đền) · evil (độc ác) · enemy (kẻ thù, giặc) · burn (đốt cháy) · underworld (âm phủ) · court (phiên tòa) · liar (kẻ nói dối) · messenger (người đưa tin)
